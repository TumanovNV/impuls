# Release QA Evidence — 1.4.12

## Evidence status

This record belongs to PR #51, the 1.4.12 release candidate. Automated Swift/Python tests, bundle verification and the production telemetry checks described in the PR remain valid evidence for their own deterministic/operational contracts, but they are **not converted into manual hardware/TCC passes** here.

The release owner completed the release-specific `UI-06` visual acceptance on a real Mac. The Menu Bar battery status was checked in light and dark appearance at Retina scale; the battery glyph, percentage, and native charging bolt were accepted without visual defects.

The other manual and mixed scenarios below were not exercised as part of this acceptance. They remain explicit `not-run` gaps and are not promoted to passes by automated CI.

## Known gaps

- `UI-06` passed its release-specific real-Mac visual acceptance. This does not certify the broader `UI-01` through `UI-05` contracts.
- The remaining manual/mixed matrix rows were not recorded as release-specific passes for this candidate and remain `not-run`. Green unit/CI checks do not upgrade them to `pass`.
- Before changing `release_decision` to `certified`, the required real-Mac/TCC/hardware scenarios must be exercised and this record updated with truthful environments/results.

## Release decision rationale

`ship-with-known-gaps` is intentional. The release-specific Menu Bar battery presentation passed `UI-06`, while the other unperformed manual/mixed scenarios remain visible as known gaps. The decision permits release without misrepresenting those scenarios as certified.
